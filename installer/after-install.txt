You're almost done!

When RigGlow opens:

  1. Go to "Setup check". It shows anything missing (OpenRGB,
     PawnIO) or anything that might fight RigGlow, with links.

  2. On the Lighting page, under "ARGB header LED count", set how
     many LEDs are on each fan header and use the test pattern to
     check it.

  3. On the Fans page, pick a fan header and choose a fixed speed
     or a temperature curve. Headers stay under motherboard control
     until you change them.

RigGlow lives in the system tray (near the clock). Right-click its
icon to exit; your fans then go back to motherboard control.

Problems or ideas: https://github.com/arkbullet2626-droid/RigGlow/issues
